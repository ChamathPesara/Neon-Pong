# ⚡ NEON PONG

> *Two paddles. One ball. Zero mercy.*

A razor-sharp reimagining of the game that started it all — stripped down, souped up, and dripping in neon. This isn't your grandfather's Pong. This is Pong with an attitude.

---

## 🌌 What Is This?

Born from a love of retro arcades and modern web craft, **Neon Pong** resurrects the world's most legendary two-button game and gives it a full glow-up. Fluid animations. A cunning AI opponent. That deeply satisfying *thwack* when the ball kisses the paddle just right.

No installs. No accounts. No nonsense.  
Just open a browser and play.

---

## 🎮 Game Modes

### 🧠 Player vs. Computer
Think you can outsmart it? The AI isn't just tracking the ball — it's *reading* you. Adjustable error margins mean it plays human enough to keep you hooked, but merciless enough to keep you humble.

### 👥 Player vs. Player
Settle it locally. Shoulder to shoulder. May the better human win.

## 🧠 Under the Hood

The whole thing runs on **pure HTML5 Canvas** — no libraries, no frameworks, no bloat. Just a tightly written game loop powered by `requestAnimationFrame`, collision physics that actually make sense, and an AI that moves like it's got somewhere to be.

```
/pong-game
├── index.html     → Structure
├── style.css      → The glow-up
├── game.js        → Where the magic lives
└── *.wav          → The sounds that make you feel it
```

Clean separation. Easy to read. Easier to hack on.
